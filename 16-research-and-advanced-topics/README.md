# 16 — Research and Advanced Topics

Topics: P/NP, NP-hardness and NP-completeness, reductions, parameterized algorithms, FPT, online algorithms, streaming algorithms, external-memory algorithms, parallel and distributed algorithms, cache-aware algorithms, succinct data structures, quantum algorithms, learning-augmented algorithms and responsible optimization.

Research workflow: define model and assumptions, establish baseline, propose algorithm, prove correctness, analyze complexity, design adversarial tests, benchmark fairly, report limitations and identify open problems.
